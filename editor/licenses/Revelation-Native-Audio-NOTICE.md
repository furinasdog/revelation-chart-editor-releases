# Third-party provenance

- The madmom audio frontend and decoders are based on the [original madmom algorithms](https://github.com/CPJKU/madmom) and compared against [OpenMIRLab madmom-infer](https://github.com/openmirlab/madmom-infer/tree/bffead9be61857fd44b2e0d3a2510d9d94c8f2d2). The [madmom-beats-port reference implementation](https://github.com/creightonlinza/madmom-beats-port) was also used to cross-check framing, filterbank generation, and the meter model. The corresponding BSD notices are in `LICENSES`.
- ONNX models are conversions of the [CPJKU madmom models](https://github.com/CPJKU/madmom_models) published in [alumkal/madmom_models at 1491c427](https://github.com/alumkal/madmom_models/tree/1491c42703e177b812a8eb49a78e49438861ae36). Their original and converted weights are subject to [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). The runtime downloads upstream bytes. This repository contains the source and checksums.
- The native Essentia adapter is linked against [MTG Essentia at f0f6c358](https://github.com/MTG/essentia/tree/f0f6c358abd133e675710ce2d5f77cc935a75eb9) and uses the [EssentiaTD Windows build overlay at a46e1aea](https://github.com/DarienBrito/EssentiaTD/tree/a46e1aeab7367916625963dbcc10302eff49a1f9). The build script adds the streaming SuperFlux peak-picker registration to that overlay. It links [libsamplerate 0.2.2](https://github.com/libsndfile/libsamplerate/tree/0.2.2) and uses Essentia's bundled KissFFT backend. Essentia and the build overlay use the GNU Affero General Public License.
- The madmom executable dynamically links [ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime) under the MIT license.
